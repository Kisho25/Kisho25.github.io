Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Satisfying Arcade.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, scores, coins, and sound and haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. Satisfying Arcade does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, files, or precise location. Removing the app may remove locally stored game information.

Advertising

Satisfying Arcade uses Google AdMob to display non-personalized advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from an IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information to provide, secure, limit, and measure advertisements.

Advertising consent or privacy choices are presented when required for a user's region. When available, advertising privacy choices can be reviewed from the app's Settings screen.

Learn more in Google's explanation of data use on partner services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Satisfying Arcade is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on the device until the app's data is cleared or the app is removed. Information processed by Google is governed by Google's retention and deletion practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
